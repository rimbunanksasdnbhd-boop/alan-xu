Method

Agents propose.
Gates verify. People decide.

Let AI do what it is good at, and keep authoritative decisions with deterministic rules and accountable people. Every system I build follows it.

01 · agents

Specialised, not general

Each agent owns one stage with a clean hand-over, which is far more reliable than one do-everything assistant.

02 · gates

Independent, not self-graded

Independent review, tests and governance rules check every stage. The AI never grades its own work.

03 · people

Accountable, not bypassed

Product owners and business experts keep the decision wherever the risk calls for it, approved in one place.

principle · platforms

Build the foundation once

BTP@MA lets claims, e-commerce, direct integration and AI share one platform, so each new use case is cheaper and faster.

principle · value

Measure value, not usage

AI adoption is tied to productivity, quality, capacity and business value, never to how often a tool was opened.
